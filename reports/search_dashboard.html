06 A/B Experiment Protocol & Offline Simulation

Statistical Protocol

  • Randomization Unit: User-level deterministic 50/50 [MD5 hash]
  • Primary Metric: Search → PDP CTR on eligible searches
  • Statistical Test: Two-proportion two-tailed z-test (α = 0.05, power = 0.80)
  • Ship Threshold: Absolute lift ≥ +1.5 pp with p < 0.05 [PRODUCT ASSUMPTION]
  • Target Effect Size (MDE): +3.5 pp (3.08% → 6.58%) [PRODUCT ASSUMPTION]
  • Modeled Runtime: ~76 calendar days at ~15.7 searches/day [MODELED]
